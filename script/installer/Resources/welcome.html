TONE3000

This installs the TONE3000 standalone app and audio plug-ins on this Mac. Pick which formats to install on the next screen.

  • Standalone → /Applications
  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • AAX (Pro Tools) → /Library/Application Support/Avid/Audio/Plug-Ins

The installer is ad-hoc signed (not Developer-ID notarized). The first time you launch it, macOS may show an “unidentified developer” warning — close it, then right-click the .pkg in Finder and choose Open, then Open in the dialog.
