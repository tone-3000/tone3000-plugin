# M2.2 ring policy, on-device observation (2026-10-17)

Scope: verify the ring under/overrun policy counters are surfaced and that
the driver still streams without URB xruns after the M2.2 changes. This is
not the M2.4 soak and it is not a latency run.

Rig: Tab S10 FE + AMS-22, probe 3 URBs x 8 packets, 48 kHz/2 ch/subslot 4,
digital loopback ON, direct monitor OFF. The probe was left running for about
three minutes and then stopped from the app (Back, so onDestroy ran
`nativeStop`).

Observed (last 5 s sample before stopping):

```
capUrb=197197 capPkts=1577576 playUrb=194223 playPkts=1553784 xruns=0
ringOver=503 ringUnder=4
capBytesPerUrb=383 playBytesPerUrb=389
fbUrb=25001 fbPkts=200008 fbErr=0 fbVal=6.00000
```

Reading:

- URB-level xruns stay 0; feedback packets stay error-free.
- `ringUnder=4` is the three playback URBs primed before the first capture
  arrived, plus one; no underruns after the stream warmed up.
- `ringOver` climbs at roughly 2-4 events/s. The capture and playback URB
  rates differ by ~1.5% on this run (986/s capture vs 967/s playback), and
  the pacer compensates by sending ~48.6 frames per playback URB (389 bytes
  per URB vs 384 nominal). The +1-frame ceiling is just enough to keep the
  audio rate matched, so the ring sits near full and periodically laps.
- This is a probe scheduling symptom, not a ring-policy failure: the policy
  drops oldest capture frames and flags the overrun as specified. M2.4's
  chosen URB layout / pump loop must bring the playback URB rate up to
  nominal so the ring is not sitting at its ceiling. Do not hide it with a
  larger ring; that spends the latency budget.

Host suite: 70/70 green (`script/test-usb.sh`), including the 10^8-frame
producer/consumer stress (109 ms) and the +1-frame pacing test that would
have caught the old scratch-buffer overrun.
